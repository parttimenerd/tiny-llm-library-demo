# JavaZone Opening Monologue

## The Prompt (from README)

```
Write a short (3-4 sentence), fun and nerdy opening monologue for a talk called
"Let's create a tiny LLM library together" at JavaZone Oslo (the largest Java
conference in Scandinavia). Thank the organizers for the excellent food and
hospitality. Tone: enthusiastic, slightly self-deprecating, technical crowd.
```

## The Monologue

> Hei hei, JavaZone! First, a huge thank-you to the organizers for the
> legendary food and hospitality — I've already consumed more calories than my
> model will have parameters, which, honestly, is exactly why we're calling it
> a *tiny* LLM. A quick confession: when the world decided machine learning
> belonged to Python, I looked at my JVM, whispered "hold my garbage
> collector," and made some architectural choices I can't fully defend. So grab
> your favorite IDE and lower your expectations to a reasonable dtype — today
> we're building an LLM library together from scratch, and by the end you'll
> either understand transformers or at least have a plausible excuse for why
> your code hallucinates.
